SPOONS

You get a tray of spoons each morning. Everything costs some. Spend them, save them, or borrow from tomorrow.

  • Pinch a spoon, drop it on a task to pay.
  • Pinch a task, toss it to Tomorrow or Let go.
  • Hold the pillow to rest. Time keeps moving.
  • Flares bend spoons. Rest straightens them.
